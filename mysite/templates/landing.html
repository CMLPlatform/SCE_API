From product data to better decisions

Digital Product Passports are usually seen as a compliance task: manufacturers collect product data and hand it over. The Sustainability and Cost Evaluator (SCE) also puts that data to work for the manufacturer.

It adds a new step to the passport life cycle, use the DPP for decisions. Process developers and key account managers compare laser welding options on the same evidence that ends up in the passport.

The SCE is the sustainability and cost module of the Lasers4MaaS platform. It supports the move to customised, flexible and decentralised manufacturing as a service.

Three things the SCE does

KPI calculation

Calculates 13 key performance indicators for every option, including costs, energy use and carbon footprint. The values reach the platform through a dedicated KPI API.

Decision support

Combines the KPIs with your preferences to rank and score the options. It handles uncertain values and weights, groups of criteria and hard limits, and shows how robust each ranking is.

Product passport

Creates the Digital Product Passport for the chosen welding set-up, with a link and QR code. The supply chain's environmental impacts are calculated as part of it.

Built for two roles

The process developer decides which process is fit for purpose. The key account manager decides where and how it runs at scale.

Research and development

Process developer

“Which successful welding experiments should we recommend for industrial use?”

  • Compare experiments that passed the platform's quality screening
  • Weigh weld quality against operating costs, energy use and process carbon footprint
  • Send the preferred experiments back to the platform

Industrial operations

Key account manager

“Which plant with a dynamic beam laser welding station should we prioritise?”

  • Compare welding stations across plants, including pre- and post-processing
  • Include total production costs, product carbon footprint, scrap rate and productivity
  • Publish the passport for the chosen set-up

13 KPIs in five categories

The indicators were agreed with the industrial partners. Each role uses the subset that matches its decision.

Costs

  • Total production costs
  • Maintenance costs

Quality

  • Process automation level
  • Operator specialisation level
  • Process monitoring

Productivity

  • Production lead time
  • Production cycle time
  • Machine saturation

Sustainability

  • Process energy consumption
  • Product carbon footprint
  • Process carbon footprint

Circularity

  • Scrap rate
  • Recyclability

How it is built

Data collection, three ways

A spreadsheet for the lab, a web application for passport data, and APIs that connect the SCE to the Lasers4MaaS platform.

Interactive decision support

Multi-criteria analysis based on PROMETHEE, extended with stochastic weights (SMAA) and veto thresholds.

Passport creator

Turns the production line into passport data: composition, components, transport and environmental impacts.

Supply chain model

A parameterised life cycle assessment model of the supply chain, reporting climate change impact with the Environmental Footprint 3.1 method.
